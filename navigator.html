How this works

The guided path follows the six steps BIS sets out in the Commerce Control List Order of Review. It does not guess — it asks the questions the regulation asks, in the order the regulation asks them, and keeps a record of your answers.

You are the classifier. Self-classification is expressly permitted by §732.3(b); the exporter of record owns the result.
