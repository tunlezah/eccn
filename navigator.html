How this works

The guided path follows the structure of the six steps BIS sets out in the Commerce Control List Order of Review — the §734.3 scope screen, the enumerated and catch-all 600-series/9x515 checks, the §772.1 test, and the full-CCL EAR99 conclusion — and keeps a record of your answers.

You are the classifier. Self-classification is expressly permitted by §732.3(b); the exporter of record owns the result.
